# WILDWIRE — approved daily edition contract

Approved by the founder on October 16, 2026. This is the current daily template;
it supersedes older creative notes when they conflict. Preserve the approved
site instead of redesigning it. Motto: **DISCOVER. INSPIRE. EVOLVE.**

## What changes every day

- Six substantial new pillar articles: STRENGTH, STYLE, EXPLORE, CULTURE, LIVING, FINANCE.
- Three additional, completely new stories in **Daily Read / Stay for the good
  part**. Show exactly three current stories here; the growing archive lives in
  All Stories, category navigation, search and readers' saved stories.
- The hero and three selections in **The lives we're building** promote the new
  edition. Hero/title/caption/links change with their selected stories.
- **Take the wider view** keeps its six category labels and layout, opens each
  pillar's new article, and uses six NEW separate tile images. Never reuse the
  hero or card cover as a pillar tile, or reuse a previous day's image.
- The Living practice/spirituality feature uses the new Living story, title,
  image and specific grounded practice; it must not retain yesterday's ending.
- Preserve all previous stories and permanent IDs/URLs. Saved favorites must
  continue pointing to the same stories. Never replace old stories in place.

The homepage intentionally links some feature stories in more than one place.
Each article has one permanent cover; the six pillar tiles have independent
images. No editorial image asset is repurposed for an unrelated article.

## Identity and pictures

Premium, inclusive, intelligent lifestyle and culture for ambitious, driven
people. Fierce and focused, with warmth and a human life beyond achievement.
Men and women belong equally. Daily generated images center different couples,
friends, partners or families. Vary ethnicities, ages, attainable bodies,
relationships, settings, faces, clothing and compositions. This is not a
publication for one gender or for loners.

Strength: serious effort, believable technique, practical attainable bodies;
no smiling at each other during a lift. The previously approved woman-deadlifting
image is an identity reference and an archived asset: do not replace/delete it.
Style: poised, confident runway energy with accessible real-world character.
Explore: purposeful curious companions and a recognizable sense of place.
Culture: attentive people questioning, looking, learning or discussing ideas.
Living: capable, human, warm; a smile belongs naturally around food and family.
Avoid excessive luxury, impossible physiques, metal-band styling and generic
smiling-stock-photo energy. Generated scenes are illustrations, never evidence
of a real event or an actual interview. Record model, prompt and image credit.

Keep `images/wildwire-wordmark.webp` exactly as approved: ivory/champagne WILDWIRE
with its red sword-like slash. Preserve the header, motto size, current fonts,
colors, buttons, spacing and responsive layout. Do not restore older serif
fonts or a gold slash because an earlier creative note mentioned them.

## Article standard

Original meaningful stories with a strong opening, distinct angle and developed
sections. Human, confident prose; no fabricated interviews, quotations,
statistics, personal experiences or source metadata. Separate commentary from
reporting. Cite exact credible pages at the claims they support. Prefer primary
sources. Verify names, titles, author/publisher attribution and time-sensitive
claims. Training needs explanation, then practical ordered exercises, sets,
reps/rest and appropriate adaptations. Recipes need usable quantities and
ordered instructions. Living covers relationships, family, food, psychology,
spirituality, home and personal growth; do not repeat a generic closing ritual.

Do not recycle yesterday's coffee, meal or shared-ambition story with a new
headline. Compare against the full article archive. No filler to meet a quota.
