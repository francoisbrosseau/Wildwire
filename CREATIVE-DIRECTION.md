# WILDWIRE — authoritative creative direction

User brief received October 15, 2026. This governs creative decisions for the publication. Improve the existing installation; do not reinstall WILDWIRE 2.0. Explain major creative decisions and show the resulting design before deployment. Ask before any important decision that conflicts with this brief.

WILDWIRE is an independent, premium digital lifestyle magazine for curious, ambitious people: men and women, singles, couples and families. Its identity is bold, warm, intelligent, confident, strong, cinematic and sophisticated. Combine powerful editorial imagery, sophisticated glamour, adventure, human connection, warmth and a little grit. Avoid generic fitness, fashion or wellness branding, corporate design, and ordinary airport-magazine styling.

Preserve the large centered WILDWIRE masthead: WILD in warm ivory, WIRE in champagne gold. Preserve DISCOVER. INSPIRE. EVOLVE.; the STRENGTH, STYLE, EXPLORE, CULTURE and LIVING pillars; dark charcoal, champagne gold, warm ivory, elegant serif headlines; spacious responsive layouts and readability.

Coverage spans strength and fitness, style, travel and adventure, books, culture, technology, relationships, friendship, family, mindfulness, habits, food, home, money and personal development. Articles should be useful, engaging, researched, credible and full of personality. Avoid shallow content, clichés and preachy self-improvement language. Aim for a publication readers visit every morning.

The original cinematic sea-overlook concept is an important visual reference. Replace running-shoe, ordinary-kitchen and other generic imagery. Use varied editorial compositions rather than a predictable card grid. Create visual intensity without making the publication exclusively masculine.

Preserve navigation, search, favorites, article pages and category archives. Test changes; distinguish functional checks from actual desktop/mobile visual verification.

Business direction: GitHub Pages hosting; wildwiremag.com purchased but not yet connected; eventual daily AI-assisted editorial production with quality controls and a reliable publishing workflow; recurring operating budget around $40 USD/month. These goals do not authorize implementing subscriptions, domain changes, paid services or automatic publishing in this design pass.
